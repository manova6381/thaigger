Description

Do you want to visit the 4 Islands close to Ao Nang in peace and quiet? Book a private 4 Islands tour by longtail boat with us! With this trip for up to 28 persons you can see the 4 Islands in your own pace. On the way we make snorkeling stops at Chicken Island and Poda Island, which are known for their beautiful underwater life. The captain will also bring you to Talay Waek at Tup Island, where you can see the beautiful white sandy strip between 2 small islands. Railay Beach and the famous Phra Nang Cave are up last for some swimming and a visit to the cave.

You can visit the spots in your own pace and go snorkeling or sightseeing as long as you want, as long as we are back at the pier by 14:00. Hotel transfers are included if you're staying in Ao Nang and available for an extra fee from other locations. Book the private 4 Islands tour by longtail boat here today!

Faq

You can either bring your own food, eat at one of the restaurants on Koh Poda, or we can provide a Thai-style lunch box (vegetarian and halal options available). The extra costs are THB 100/person. Please leave a remark on checkout if you want to include food to the booking.

For safety reasons we recommend that pregnant guests avoid the snorkeling stops and speak with our team before booking so we can advise on the best option for you.

The dry season from November to April offers the calmest seas and clearest visibility for snorkeling, though the tour runs daily year-round.

Yes, children of all ages are welcome. Life vests are provided in child sizes and the minimum age is 1+.

Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before the start of the tour for a full refund.

A small deposit secures your private boat, with the remaining balance payable on the day of the tour.
